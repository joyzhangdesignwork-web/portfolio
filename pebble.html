Background

The Market

AI voice coaching is a largely untapped space in China. Existing products either rely on underpowered models that lack real conversational depth, or are extensions of offline businesses — not built for the medium.

The Problem

Existing products respond to you. Pebble is designed to understand you. The difference is a three-layer dialogue framework grounded in CBT, ACT, and mindfulness — rarely seen in domestic products.

The Positioning

Positioned as a coaching tool, not a medical product — a deliberate decision to navigate China's regulatory landscape while maintaining product integrity and user safety.

The Users

User 01

The Overwhelmed Professional

Mid-20s to 30s, high-functioning but emotionally stretched. Not in crisis, but struggling with anxiety, burnout, or relationship stress. Skeptical of therapy — looking for something accessible and private.

User 02

The Self-Growth Seeker

Interested in psychology and self-improvement. Already uses journaling or meditation apps. Wants depth — not just a mood tracker, but something that helps them understand their patterns over time.

Other Key Decisions

Safety

Crisis handling without medical overreach

When a user mentions self-harm, the product surfaces a crisis resource card and gently redirects — without diagnosing, escalating, or breaking conversational trust. Designed specifically to operate within a coaching, not clinical, frame.

Monetisation

Two revenue paths designed in parallel

Structured courses (e.g. 12 Steps to Anxiety Relief) provide a scalable content revenue stream. A therapist marketplace model — where Pebble takes platform commission — creates a second path that extends the product's value without requiring clinical licensing.

Next Steps

Voice emotion recognition

Beta users flagged that the AI sometimes misreads tone — responding to "I'm fine" without catching that it was said through tears. The next design challenge is surfacing prosodic signals to the model, so the product responds to how something is said, not just what.

Monetisation flow

The revenue paths are defined — structured courses and a therapist marketplace. The next step is designing the end-to-end purchase flow and validating willingness to pay with real beta users as quickly as possible.
